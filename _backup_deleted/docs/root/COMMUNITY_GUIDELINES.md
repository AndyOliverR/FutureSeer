# 🛡️ FutureSeer Community Guidelines
*Creating a Safe, Respectful, and Enlightening Mystical Community*

## 🌟 **Our Community Philosophy**

FutureSeer is a platform where seekers of cosmic wisdom come together to learn, share, and grow. Our community is built on the principles of **respect, safety, and independent knowledge sharing**. We believe in creating an environment where everyone feels safe to explore their spiritual journey.

---

## 🎯 **Core Values**

### **1. Safety First**
- **Mental Health Protection**: We prioritize the mental and emotional well-being of all community members
- **Zero Tolerance for Harm**: Any content that could cause harm to individuals is immediately removed
- **Crisis Prevention**: Proactive monitoring and intervention for vulnerable individuals

### **2. Expert Independence**
- **Independent Contributors**: Experts are community members, not employees
- **Voluntary Participation**: Experts contribute freely to help others
- **No Jurisdiction**: FutureSeer does not control or manage expert services
- **Freedom of Expression**: Experts can create threads and discussions freely

### **3. Respectful Discourse**
- **Inclusive Environment**: Welcome to all spiritual paths and beliefs
- **Constructive Dialogue**: Disagreements are handled with respect
- **No Harassment**: Zero tolerance for bullying or targeting individuals
- **Professional Conduct**: Maintain appropriate language and behavior

---

## 🚫 **Strictly Prohibited Content**

### **Immediate Removal Offenses:**
- **Suicide Promotion**: Any content encouraging self-harm or suicide
- **Mental Health Targeting**: Targeting vulnerable individuals with harmful advice
- **Profanity & Vulgarity**: Inappropriate language or explicit content
- **Harassment**: Bullying, stalking, or targeting specific individuals
- **False Medical Claims**: Unsubstantiated health or medical advice
- **Scams & Fraud**: Attempts to deceive or defraud community members
- **Hate Speech**: Discrimination based on race, religion, gender, etc.
- **Personal Information**: Sharing private information without consent

### **Zero Tolerance Policy:**
- **First Offense**: Immediate content removal and warning email
- **Second Offense**: 30-day suspension with detailed explanation
- **Third Offense**: Permanent ban with final explanation email
- **Severe Violations**: Immediate permanent ban (suicide promotion, targeting vulnerable individuals)

1. **Content Removal**: Immediate removal of violating content
2. **Email Notification**: Detailed explanation of the violation
3. **Policy Reference**: Specific reference to violated guidelines
4. **Appeal Process**: Information on how to appeal the decision

### **Suspension Process:**
1. **Temporary Suspension**: 30-day suspension for repeated violations
2. **Detailed Explanation**: Comprehensive email explaining the suspension
3. **Appeal Rights**: Clear process for appealing the suspension
4. **Reinstatement**: Conditions for returning to the community

### **Permanent Ban:**

3. **No Appeal**: Permanent bans are final and non-appealable
4. **Legal Compliance**: Cooperation with law enforcement if necessary
