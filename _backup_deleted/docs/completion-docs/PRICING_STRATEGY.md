# FutureSeer Pricing Strategy - India-Focused

## 🌟 **Market Analysis for India**

### **Economic Context:**
- **Average Monthly Income (Urban India):** ₹25,000 - ₹50,000
- **Purchasing Power Parity (PPP):** 1 USD ≈ ₹25-30 in purchasing power
- **Digital Payment Adoption:** High (UPI, digital wallets)
- **Spiritual/Mystical Market:** Large and growing (₹1,000+ crores annually)

### **Competitive Landscape:**
- **Traditional Astrologers:** ₹500 - ₹5,000 per session
- **Online Platforms:** ₹99 - ₹999 per month
- **Mobile Apps:** ₹49 - ₹299 per month
- **YouTube/Free Content:** Abundant but low quality
