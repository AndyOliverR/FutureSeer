# Runic Meditation and Galdr Practice

## Overview

Runic meditation is the practice of connecting with individual runes through focused contemplation, body postures (stadha), vocalization (galdr), and visualization. This practice deepens the reader's intuitive connection to the runes, moving beyond intellectual understanding into embodied knowing.

## The Three Pillars

### 1. Stadha (Runic Body Postures)

Each rune has a corresponding body posture that mirrors its shape. By assuming the posture, the practitioner becomes the rune, channeling its energy through the body.

**First Aett:**
- **Fehu (ᚠ)**: Stand upright, both arms raised above the head at 45° angles, palms forward. Like antlers or branches reaching for abundance.
- **Uruz (ᚢ)**: Bend forward from the waist with arms hanging down, back curved. The arch of primal strength.
- **Thurisaz (ᚦ)**: Stand sideways, one arm bent at the elbow pointing forward like a thorn. The active defense posture.
- **Ansuz (ᚨ)**: Stand with one arm raised diagonally, the other lowered diagonally. The windswept tree receiving divine communication.
- **Raidho (ᚱ)**: Stand with one leg bent forward at the knee, as if mid-stride. The traveler in motion.
- **Kenaz (ᚲ)**: Stand with one arm bent outward at the elbow, forming the torch angle. Illumination held to the side.
- **Gebo (ᚷ)**: Stand with legs apart and arms raised diagonally, forming an X. The cross of equal exchange.
- **Wunjo (ᚹ)**: Stand upright with one arm raised high, flag-like. The banner of joy.

**Second Aett:**
- **Hagalaz (ᚺ)**: Stand with both arms extended horizontally, hands at shoulder height. The crossbar of unavoidable change.
- **Nauthiz (ᚾ)**: Stand with one arm crossing the chest diagonally. The bow drill of need-fire.
- **Isa (ᛁ)**: Stand perfectly straight, arms at sides, feet together. Complete stillness. The ice pillar.
- **Jera (ᛃ)**: Dynamic posture — alternate stepping forward and back in a slow rhythm. The cycle of the year.
- **Eihwaz (ᛇ)**: Stand on one leg, the other bent behind. Arms at sides. The rooted yew.
- **Perthro (ᛈ)**: Sit cross-legged with cupped hands resting in the lap, as if holding dice or lots. The well of mystery.
- **Algiz (ᛉ)**: Stand with both arms raised and spread, head tilted back. The elk sedge reaching skyward. Classic protection stance.
- **Sowilo (ᛊ)**: Stand with body slightly twisted, one arm reaching forward-up and the other back-down. The lightning bolt of the sun.

**Third Aett:**
- **Tiwaz (ᛏ)**: Stand with arms raised together above the head, forming a single point. The arrow pointing to the sky.
- **Berkano (ᛒ)**: Stand with arms curved at the sides, as if cradling. The pregnant birch.
- **Ehwaz (ᛖ)**: Stand with feet parallel, arms extended forward, palms touching. Two as one — the partnership posture.
- **Mannaz (ᛗ)**: Stand with arms crossed at the chest, hands on opposite shoulders. Self-contained humanity.
- **Laguz (ᛚ)**: Stand with one arm raised forward at an angle, like water flowing downhill. The wave.
- **Ingwaz (ᛝ)**: Sit or stand with hands clasped over the lower abdomen. The seed within.
- **Dagaz (ᛞ)**: Stand with arms crossed in front forming an hourglass shape, then uncross. The butterfly of dawn.
- **Othala (ᛟ)**: Stand with feet apart, arms forming a diamond shape over the chest. The ancestral estate.

### 2. Galdr (Runic Vocalization)

Galdr is the chanting or singing of rune names and sounds. Traditional galdr uses the phonetic value of the rune, sustained as a prolonged vocalization.

**Basic Galdr Method:**
1. Breathe deeply three times.
2. On the exhale, sound the rune's name slowly: "Feeee-huuuu" (Fehu), "Uuuu-ruuuuz" (Uruz).
3. Feel the vibration in the body. Each rune resonates in a different area:
   - Fehu: upper chest, throat
   - Uruz: lower abdomen, root
   - Thurisaz: solar plexus, back
   - Ansuz: throat, jaw, ears
   - Raidho: hips, legs
   - Kenaz: forehead, eyes
   - Gebo: heart center, arms
   - Wunjo: crown, whole body
4. Repeat 3, 9, or 27 times.

**Advanced Galdr:**
- Sustain the vowel sound of the rune for an entire exhale.
- Experiment with pitch — lower tones for earth runes (Fehu, Uruz, Berkano), higher for air/fire runes (Ansuz, Sowilo, Dagaz).
- Combine stadha with galdr: assume the posture while chanting. This is the full runic meditation practice.

### 3. Visualization (Runic Imagery)

Each rune carries elemental and color associations for visualization:

- **Fire runes** (Fehu, Kenaz, Sowilo, Dagaz): Visualize red-gold light, flickering flames, the sun.
- **Ice/water runes** (Isa, Hagalaz, Laguz): Visualize blue-white light, flowing water, crystalline structures.
- **Earth runes** (Uruz, Berkano, Jera, Ingwaz, Othala): Visualize green or brown light, roots, soil, growing things.
- **Air/spirit runes** (Ansuz, Raidho, Ehwaz, Mannaz): Visualize white or silver light, wind, birds, open sky.

## The 24-Day Rune Meditation Cycle

A structured program for deepening runic connection:

**Days 1–8 (First Aett — Freya's Aett):**
One rune per day, from Fehu through Wunjo. Focus on the themes of material existence, survival, and basic human needs.

**Days 9–16 (Second Aett — Heimdall's Aett):**
Hagalaz through Sowilo. Themes of challenge, transformation, and the forces that test us.

**Days 17–24 (Third Aett — Tiwaz's Aett):**
Tiwaz through Othala. Themes of social order, spiritual development, and integration with community and ancestry.

**Daily Practice (20–30 minutes):**
1. Draw the rune of the day on paper or in sand.
2. Assume the stadha for 3–5 minutes.
3. Chant the galdr 9 times.
4. Sit in quiet meditation with the rune's image in your mind's eye for 10 minutes.
5. Journal any impressions, emotions, or insights.

## Integration with Divination

Practitioners who maintain a regular rune meditation practice report:
- Faster, more intuitive readings.
- Deeper understanding of nuance when runes appear in combination.
- Ability to "feel" the energy of a rune cast before intellectually analyzing it.
- Clearer communication with the runes — a sense of dialogue rather than mechanical interpretation.

The meditation practice is recommended before any significant rune reading, as a way to center and attune.
